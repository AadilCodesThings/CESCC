About

Who we are

Consolidated Engineering & Sourcing Consultants (CESC) is a Dubai-based engineering, EPC and equipment sourcing firm serving utilities, EPC contractors, developers and industrial clients across the Middle East, Africa and South Asia.

We combine senior power systems engineering with direct access to qualified manufacturers. That means a client can bring us a single-line diagram, a tender BOQ or an outline requirement and receive a technically reviewed, commercially firm offer for medium and low voltage equipment, with engineering, factory testing, logistics and site support handled by one team.

Our engineers have designed, validated, manufactured and commissioned MV/HV switchgear, transformers and substations for over three decades, working inside global OEMs and licensed manufacturing plants before founding CESC. That background is what allows us to review a manufacturer’s design critically, negotiate on technical substance and stand behind what we supply.

Deliver

What we deliver

Engineering, licensed product supply, EPC execution and thermal power—scoped for utilities, EPCs, developers and industrial clients.

Equipment supply and sourcing

Power and distribution transformers, MV and LV switchgear, ring main units, packaged and compact substations, low voltage panels, circuit breakers and spares. We qualify manufacturers, review designs against the specification, witness factory acceptance tests and manage delivery to site in the region.

Westinghouse LV — licensed partner

CESC holds the Westinghouse LVMB licence covering EMEA, CIS, the United Kingdom and Australia. We supply Westinghouse low voltage circuit breakers and assemblies, support licensed local assemblers and provide type-tested LV solutions.

Substation design from 13.8 kV to 380 kV, switchgear specification, system studies, protection philosophy and relay coordination, earthing and short circuit studies, and engineering review of vendor documents.

EPC and project execution

Bid preparation, engineering procurement and construction strategy, subcontractor management, testing and commissioning supervision and energisation support for substations, distribution networks and power plants.

Renewable energy and storage

Solar PV plants, battery energy storage systems and hybrid PV, storage and thermal plants. Scope covers MV collection systems, inverter and transformer stations, grid connection, BESS integration and EXW or delivered packages.

Thermal and rental power

Feasibility, engine selection and sourcing of new and used HFO, diesel and dual-fuel generating sets from single units to multi-unit plants in the tens of megawatts, including containerised rental power.

Process

How we work

What happens after you send an enquiry—from clarification to delivery and support.

  1. 01

    Enquiry and clarification

    Send us a BOQ, single-line diagram, tender specification or a description of the need. We confirm scope, standards and site conditions within two working days.

  2. 02

    Engineering review

    We check ratings, short circuit levels, ambient derating, protection scheme and interfaces before pricing so the offer matches the real requirement, not just the line items.

  3. 03

    Sourcing and offer

    Qualified manufacturers are selected for the application, origin requirements and budget. The client receives a single consolidated technical and commercial offer with deviations clearly listed.

  4. 04

    Manufacturing and testing

    Drawing approval, inspection, routine and type test review and factory acceptance testing witnessed by our engineers or the client.

  5. 05

    Delivery and support

    Packing, shipping, documentation, site supervision, commissioning support and spares over the life of the equipment.

Quality

Standards and quality

Specification, type testing and site-condition verification before equipment is released.

  • Equipment engineered and specified to IEC 62271, IEC 61439, IEC 60076, IEC 60255 and, where required, ANSI/IEEE and utility-specific standards such as SEC, DEWA and ADDC.
  • Type test certificates from recognised laboratories reviewed for every switchgear and transformer package before release.
  • Routine test reports, factory acceptance test protocols and as-built documentation supplied with every order.
  • Internal arc classification, short circuit withstand and ambient derating verified against the actual site conditions, not catalogue values.
  • Design failure mode analysis and reliability review applied to new or modified product designs.
