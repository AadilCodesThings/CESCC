Who we are

Consolidated Engineering and Sourcing Consultants is a senior power systems engineering consultancy with 33 years of cumulative international experience in the design, validation, manufacturing, commissioning, and commercialization of medium and high voltage electrical infrastructure.

The firm’s expertise spans the complete lifecycle of power distribution systems—from conceptual design, system studies, protection coordination, type testing, and compliance validation to manufacturing engineering, operational optimization, and EPC execution strategy.

The consultancy has led regional portfolios exceeding $100M in annual business across the Middle East, Africa, Turkey, and Pakistan, delivering substation and power plant projects up to 2000 MW and 380 kV class systems.
